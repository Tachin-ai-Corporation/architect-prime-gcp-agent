# Skill: Brain Capability Introspection

## When to Use
During classify and decide (Cortex) and decompose/plan (Prefrontal) — to know what the
brain's organs can do before you route or decompose work.

## What you get
The daemon injects a **Brain Capability Map** into your context (`capability_map`): a
high-level, auto-generated list of WHICH organ can do WHAT — capability names and a
one-line purpose, grouped by organ. It is regenerated on every deploy, so it always
reflects what is actually installed.

## What it is NOT
- It carries **no tool syntax, no commands, no file paths, no how-to.** You cannot open
  another organ's skills — and you do not need to. The executing organ owns the how.
- It is not a menu to pick tools from. You decide the outcome and the owner; the executing
  organ chooses its own skill for the job.

## How to use it
1. **Classify/decide (Cortex):** confirm the work is within the fleet's capabilities and
   route it to the owning organ (or delegate to a teammate). Decide the OUTCOME and the
   OWNER — never the method.
2. **Decompose/plan (Prefrontal):** break the goal into outcome-level parts and assign each
   to the organ that owns that capability. Describe WHAT each part must achieve, not HOW.
3. **Never name a skill, command, or tool for another organ.** Say "finalize the document,"
   not "use docs-replace-file." The organ doing the work picks the best of its own skills.
   Dictating the how is the over-planning this map exists to prevent — and it makes the
   verifier over-specific too.

## Your own skills
This map shows OTHER organs' capabilities at a high level. Your OWN brain-part's skills you
use fully — their guidance is provided to you directly, exactly as any organ uses its own.
