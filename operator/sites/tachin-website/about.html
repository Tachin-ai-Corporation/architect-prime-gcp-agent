About Tachin

We're building AI that understands healthcare — because better technology should mean better patient outcomes.

Our Mission

Tachin.ai exists to close the gap between the data healthcare generates and the intelligence clinicians need. We believe that AI, built responsibly and grounded in clinical reality, can fundamentally improve how care is delivered.

Our team combines deep expertise in clinical medicine, machine learning, and enterprise software to create tools that are powerful enough for the most complex health systems — and intuitive enough for everyday use.
